I don't give a shit what you do with this mod. Distribute it, make it your own, whatever. As long as you're having fun, we're cool.

WarcraftXL community runtime (DragonUI, Immersion, DialogUI, etc.),
plus our modified camera-side extensions. Existing upstream license
and attribution files are left intact. The source-only release
includes the latest locally available WXL core baseline source,
authored modifications and license files in the corresponding trees.

Eclipse dinput8.dll runtime, account credentials/roster, or server
repack binary is packaged. The optional WXL Eclipse bridge is our own
customization; it requires users to provide their own compatible
