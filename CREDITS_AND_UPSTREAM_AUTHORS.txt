ACTION CAMERA SUITE: CREATORS, ATTRIBUTION AND UPSTREAM SOURCES
==============================================================
We distribute an integrated camera-oriented WoW 3.3.5a mod pack
built on the work of the following original developers.

IMMERSION
  Original creator: Sebastian Lindfors (seblindfors)
  Original project: https://github.com/seblindfors/Immersion
  Original Immersion quest-and-gossip interface code and assets
  remain credited to the upstream project.
  Bundled license: Interface/AddOns/Immersion/LICENSE.md

DRAGONUI
  Original creator / 3.3.5a maintainer: NeticSoul
  Original project: https://github.com/NeticSoul/DragonUI
  DragonUI and the DragonUI_Options UI framework are upstream work.
  Additional borrowed/adapted assets, libraries and contributors
  are documented in DragonUI/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and LICENSES.
  Bundled license: Interface/AddOns/DragonUI/LICENSE.txt
  Their third-party art/fonts/libraries retain their original terms.

DIALOGUI
  Original creator: Sxus (original DialogUI).
  Original source: https://github.com/Jslquintero/DialogUI
  Wrath of the Lich King port: Bset.
  WotLK port source: https://github.com/ghbset/DialogUI-WotLK
  Bundled fork and 3.3.5a adaptation: Stefan2102.
  Included edition is the book-reader / Immersion compatibility fork.
  See Interface/AddOns/DialogUI/README.md, the DialogUI .toc
  and its bundled MIT LICENSE for original attribution and terms.

SEYRIS TOOLS
  Original module creator and repository maintainer: Morfium-G.
  Source repository: https://github.com/Morfium-G/wxl-seyris-tools
  This shared toolkit is a separate WarcraftXL v1.1 extension,
  providing cdbc handling, editing, settings and crash reports.
  Its original authorship is credited independently of our
  custom WXL integration.
  A separate redistribution license was not identified in
  the upstream repository; confirm permission before wide release.

WARCRAFTXL FRAMEWORK
  Original open-source project: WarcraftXL and contributors.
  Core source: https://github.com/WarcraftXL/wxl-core
  Framework: GNU General Public License, version 3.
  We distribute adaptations of the framework, not a claim
  to have created the upstream WXL runtime.
